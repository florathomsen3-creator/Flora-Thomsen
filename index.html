Why We Exist

The publishing world has changed dramatically over the past decade. Today, writing a remarkable book is only half the journey. The other half is making sure the right readers know it exists. That is where QUILL PATHWAY LITERARY steps in.

We understand both the creative and commercial sides of the book world, and we use that understanding to build campaigns that feel authentic to each author's voice and vision.

We are not interested in quick fixes or inflated metrics. We care about building sustainable reader relationships that grow over time and support your career as an author for the long term.

Our Approach

Finding Readers, Not Just Impressions

Many marketing services focus on vanity metrics: impressions, reach, clicks. While those numbers have their place, they do not tell the full story. At QUILL PATHWAY LITERARY, we focus on genuine reader discovery. That means putting your book in front of people who are likely to read it, enjoy it, and recommend it to others.

Our discovery process begins with deep audience research. We study your genre, your comparable titles, and the reading habits of your ideal audience. From there, we identify the specific channels, communities, and platforms where those readers are most active and most receptive to new book recommendations.

The result is a focused, efficient campaign that prioritizes quality of engagement over sheer volume of exposure.

Genre-Specific Targeting

We do not use a one-size-fits-all approach. A literary fiction reader discovers books differently than a romance reader or a thriller enthusiast. We tailor every campaign to the discovery habits of your specific genre audience.

Community-Driven Outreach

Readers trust recommendations from their communities. We engage with book clubs, reading groups, genre forums, and social media communities where authentic word-of-mouth drives discovery.

Multi-Channel Coordination

Reader discovery rarely happens through a single touchpoint. We coordinate across blogs, social media, newsletters, podcasts, and retail platforms to create multiple opportunities for readers to encounter your book.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions we hear most often from authors considering our services.

Home / FAQ
What types of authors do you work with?
We work with authors across a wide range of genres including literary fiction, commercial fiction, romance, mystery and thriller, fantasy, non-fiction, memoir, and more. Our approach is tailored to each author's specific genre and audience, so there is no single type of writer we specialize in exclusively. What matters most is that you take your craft seriously and want a marketing partner who will do the same.
Do you work with both self-published and traditionally published authors?
Yes. We work with authors across all publishing models including self-published, traditionally published, hybrid-published, and small-press authors. Our services are designed to complement whatever publishing path you have chosen and to fill the gaps in marketing support that are common across the industry.
How long does it take to see results?
Meaningful marketing results take time. Some campaigns show early indicators within the first few weeks, but building real reader momentum typically unfolds over three to six months of consistent, strategic effort. We are honest about this from the start because sustainable growth cannot be rushed. Short-term spikes rarely translate into long-term career success.
Can you guarantee bestseller status or specific sales numbers?
No, and we would encourage you to be cautious of any marketing service that makes such promises. Book sales depend on many factors including the book itself, the market conditions, reader interest, timing, and countless other variables outside any marketer's control. What we can guarantee is professional, strategic work executed with care and transparency.
How much do your services cost?
Our services are priced based on the scope and complexity of each engagement. We offer both project-based and retainer options to accommodate different needs and budgets. After an initial consultation, we provide a detailed proposal with transparent pricing so you can make an informed decision.
What does a typical engagement look like?
Most engagements begin with a strategy development phase followed by implementation across the agreed channels. We provide regular reporting, hold periodic strategy calls, and continuously optimize based on performance data. The exact structure depends on which services you choose and your goals.
Do I need to have a large existing following to work with you?
Not at all. We work with authors at every career stage, including debut writers with no existing platform. In fact, helping authors build their platforms from the ground up is one of the things we do most frequently. What matters is your commitment to the long-term work of connecting with readers.
How involved do I need to be in the process?
Your involvement depends on the services you choose. Some authors prefer a hands-off approach where we handle most of the day-to-day work, while others want to be deeply involved in strategy and content decisions. We adapt to your preferences and availability while ensuring you always know what is happening with your campaigns.
Can you help with international markets?
Yes. While many of our clients focus on English-language markets in North America, the UK, and Australia, we can develop strategies that target international audiences depending on your book's reach and translation availability.
What is your refund policy?
We offer a transparent refund policy that depends on the stage of your project and the services engaged. Please visit our Refund Policy page for complete details on eligibility, timelines, and the process for requesting a refund.
How do I get started?
The first step is simple: reach out through our contact form or email us directly. We will schedule a free discovery call to learn about you and your book, answer your questions, and determine whether we are the right fit for your needs. There is no pressure and no obligation.

Home / Refund Policy
At QUILL PATHWAY LITERARY, we believe in fair, transparent business practices. This Refund Policy outlines when and how refunds are available for our services. Please read carefully before engaging our services.

Overview

We offer customized marketing services that often involve significant upfront research, planning, and coordination. Because our work begins immediately upon engagement, our refund terms reflect the stage of work completed at the time of a refund request.

Eligibility for Refunds

Services Not Yet Commenced

If you request a refund before any work has begun on your project, you are eligible for a full refund of the amount paid, less any non-refundable deposits or third-party costs already incurred on your behalf (such as advertising spend or platform fees).

Services In Progress

If work has already begun, you may be eligible for a partial refund based on the portion of services not yet delivered. We will calculate this based on completed deliverables, hours worked, and any external costs committed to your campaign. All partial refunds are reviewed case by case.

Services Completed

Once services have been fully delivered, they are not eligible for refund. However, if you believe a deliverable did not meet the agreed scope, we are committed to working with you to address the concern through revisions or alternative solutions.

Monthly Retainers

For ongoing retainer-based engagements, cancellation and refund terms are as follows:

  • You may cancel a retainer with 30 days' written notice
  • Refunds are not provided for the current billing cycle once work has commenced
  • Prorated adjustments may be considered for undelivered work in future billing cycles
  • Any pre-paid retainer months not yet used may be eligible for refund upon review

How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, please contact us directly through the following steps:

  • Email us at hello@quillpathwayliterary.com with the subject line "Refund Request"
  • Include your full name, project details, and the reason for your request
  • Allow up to 10 business days for our team to review and respond
  • Approved refunds will be processed within 14 business days to the original payment method

Non-Refundable Items

The following are not eligible for refund under any circumstances:

  • Third-party advertising spend already deployed
  • Platform or software fees paid on your behalf
  • Services delivered and accepted
  • Consultation sessions already conducted

Our Commitment

We are committed to resolving any concerns before they reach the point of a refund request. If you are ever unsatisfied with our work, please reach out immediately. In most cases, we can address the issue through revisions, adjusted strategy, or additional support. Our goal is a successful, lasting partnership, not a transactional relationship.
